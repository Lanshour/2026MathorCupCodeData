## 数据说明（中文原文）

西藏日喀则市吉隆县地处喜马拉雅山南缘，地形高差大、山地地形复杂，极端天气及由其诱发或加剧的山洪、泥石流、滑坡等自然灾害风险较高。2026年8月26日，吉隆县吉隆口岸发生重大泥石流灾害，造成重大人员伤亡，引发了社会各界对喜马拉雅高寒山区极端天气与自然灾害风险的广泛关注。此次灾害也进一步凸显了高寒山区开展长期连续气象观测、加强灾害天气监测与科学研究的重要性。

为充分发挥长期科学观测资料的社会和科研价值，本数据集整理并开放共享西藏日喀则市吉隆县高寒山区气象塔2021–2026年的逐小时连续观测资料，旨在为科研机构、高校、相关业务部门及防灾减灾领域用户提供可获取、可利用的基础气象观测数据。一方面，数据可用于分析吉隆及其周边高寒山区大气边界层结构、天气过程及极端气象事件特征，为认识复杂山区天气系统演变及其环境背景提供长期观测依据；另一方面，可与卫星遥感、再分析资料、天气预报产品和数值模式结果相结合，为区域极端天气监测、灾害天气过程分析、风险识别及防灾减灾研究提供基础数据支撑。

本数据集观测站位于吉隆县城（北纬28.86°，东经85.29°，海拔4140m），距离受灾区吉隆口岸直线距离约65km。数据包含5层（1.0、2.0、4.0、10.0、20.0米）风速风向、气温、相对湿度，近地面气压、降水、辐射四分量，以及5层土壤水热参数（0.1、0.2、0.4、0.8和1.6米）。数据时间范围为2021年4月26日至2026年8月27日，时间分辨率为1小时，数据时间采用北京时间（UTC+8），各观测记录按照统一时间格式进行整理。对于原始数据中的缺测、异常或无效记录，按照统一质量控制标准进行标识和处理（0：正确，1：可疑，2：错误，4：插补数据，8：数据缺失，9：未进行质量控制）。

需要说明的是，本数据集不宜直接用于表征灾区局地降水及其他微尺度气象条件，也不用于直接判定此次泥石流的触发机制。但是，长期连续观测资料能够从区域大气环境和边界层过程角度，为认识吉隆地区极端天气活动、大气热力和动力结构变化以及高寒山区自然灾害天气背景提供重要参考，并可用于展示长期科学观测在重大自然灾害事件研究中的潜在应用价值。

## 数据说明（英文原文）

Gyirong County, located in Shigatse City along the southern margin of the Himalayas in Tibet, is characterized by large elevation differences and highly complex mountainous terrain. The region is exposed to considerable risks from extreme weather events and associated natural hazards, including flash floods, debris flows, and landslides. On 26 August 2026, a major debris-flow disaster occurred at the Gyirong Port in Gyirong County, resulting in significant casualties and attracting widespread public attention to the risks associated with extreme weather and natural hazards in the high-altitude mountainous regions of the Himalayas. This event further highlights the importance of long-term continuous meteorological observations and strengthened monitoring and scientific research on hazardous weather processes in high-altitude mountainous regions.

To maximize the scientific and societal value of long-term observations, this dataset compiles and openly provides hourly continuous meteorological observations collected from a meteorological tower in the high-altitude mountainous region of Gyirong County, Shigatse, Tibet, covering the period from 2021 to 2026. The dataset is intended to provide accessible and usable basic meteorological observations for research institutions, universities, operational agencies, and users engaged in disaster prevention and mitigation. The observations can be used to investigate the structure of the atmospheric boundary layer, weather processes, and characteristics of extreme weather events in Gyirong and the surrounding high-altitude mountainous regions, thereby providing long-term observational evidence for understanding the evolution of weather systems and their environmental conditions over complex mountainous terrain. In addition, the observations can be integrated with satellite remote sensing products, reanalysis datasets, weather forecast products, and numerical model simulations to support regional extreme-weather monitoring, analysis of hazardous weather processes, risk identification, and research on disaster prevention and mitigation.

The observation station is located in Gyirong County (28.26°N, 85.29°E; elevation: 4140 m), approximately 65 km in a straight-line distance from Gyirong Port, where the aforementioned disaster occurred. The dataset includes wind speed and wind direction, air temperature, and relative humidity observed at five levels (1.0, 2.0, 4.0, 10.0, and 20.0 m); near-surface atmospheric pressure; precipitation; four components of radiation; and soil hydrothermal parameters at five depths (0.1, 0.2, 0.4, 0.8, and 1.6 m). The dataset covers the period from 26 April 2021 to 27 August 2026, with a temporal resolution of 1 hour. All timestamps are reported in Beijing Time (UTC+8), and the observations have been organized using a standardized time format. Missing, anomalous, or invalid records in the original observations have been identified and processed according to unified quality-control criteria, with quality-control flags defined as follows: 0, correct; 1, questionable; 2, erroneous; 4, interpolated; 8, missing; and 9, quality control not performed.

It should be noted that this dataset is not intended to directly characterize local precipitation or other microscale meteorological conditions at the disaster site, nor should it be used to directly determine the triggering mechanism of the debris flow that occurred on 26 August 2026. Nevertheless, the long-term continuous observations provide important information on the regional atmospheric environment and boundary-layer processes, which can contribute to understanding extreme weather activity, changes in atmospheric thermodynamic and dynamic structures, and the meteorological background of natural hazards in the high-altitude mountainous region of Gyirong. The dataset also demonstrates the potential value of long-term scientific observations for investigating major natural hazard events.
